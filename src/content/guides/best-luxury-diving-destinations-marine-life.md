## Choose the animal, then choose the destination

If marine life is your reason for diving, start with the encounter you hope to have. Sharks, rays, turtles and pelagics require different environments and seasons.

Do not treat wildlife as a guaranteed attraction. The ocean does not operate on a timetable.

## Fuvahmulah and the Maldives

Visit Maldives highlights Fuvahmulah for pelagic diving and notes several shark species found around the island. [The Maldives](/destinations/maldives/) is also associated with manta rays and whale sharks in certain atolls and seasons.

## Moorea and French Polynesia

Tahiti Tourisme describes Moorea's lagoons as rich in marine life, with stingrays, turtles, blacktip sharks and lemon sharks among the animals divers and snorkellers may encounter. Farther out, [French Polynesia's](/destinations/french-polynesia/) Tuamotu passes are known for reef sharks.

## Other encounters worth planning around

- **Manta rays and whale sharks** — [Mozambique](/destinations/mozambique/) (Tofo) and [the Maldives](/destinations/maldives/).
- **Mobula rays and blue sharks** — [the Azores](/destinations/azores/) in summer.
- **Sardine shoals and thresher sharks** — [the Philippines](/destinations/philippines/).
- **Turtles and dolphins in a protected park** — [Fernando de Noronha](/destinations/fernando-de-noronha/).

## What makes a marine-life trip memorable

The best encounter is not necessarily the rarest animal. It is the one where the conditions are right, the guide knows how to behave around wildlife and you have enough time to watch rather than chase.

Choose operators that prioritise animal welfare and local conservation rules. Read our guide to [reef conservation](/guides/luxury-ocean-travel-reef-conservation/).
