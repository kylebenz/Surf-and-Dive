## Two excellent winter escapes

If you live in Europe and want ocean time without a long-haul flight, [Madeira](/destinations/madeira/) and the [Canary Islands](/destinations/canary-islands/) deserve serious attention.

The choice comes down to whether you want more variety across islands and surf zones or a compact, dramatic island with strong luxury and nature appeal.

## Madeira: dramatic and refined

Madeira's tourism board describes year-round surf potential and highlights a range of locations across the island.

The island is particularly good for a mixed-interest couple: surf, hiking, food, viewpoints and comfortable hotels all fit together.

## Canaries: surf variety

The Canary Islands offer multiple islands and a strong surf culture, making them useful when you want to choose your coastline around conditions and travel style.

## Which should you choose?

Choose Madeira for scenery, compact logistics and a more lush, mountainous feel. Choose the Canaries when surf choice and island variety are the priority. The full criteria are in [Canary Islands vs Madeira](/compare/canary-islands-vs-madeira/).

## The European advantage

Neither requires you to spend half your holiday recovering from the flight. That matters. A four- or five-night ocean escape can still feel worthwhile.
