## Christmas by the ocean changes the mood

There is something slightly strange about spending December in cold weather while looking at photos of tropical water.

If your ideal Christmas is warm sand, an early surf and dinner outside, the ocean offers plenty of alternatives.

## Maldives for pure island luxury

[The Maldives](/destinations/maldives/) is built around the idea of the island escape, with reef activities and premium resort experiences. Its surf season extends across much of the year.

## Fiji for the active traveller

[Fiji](/destinations/fiji/) works when the holiday needs more movement. The Mamanuca Islands combine surf, water activities and island stays, with Cloudbreak attracting serious surfers. December falls outside the peak surf season, so treat surf as a bonus.

## French Polynesia for a cinematic New Year

Moorea and other islands offer lagoons, mountain scenery and underwater life. Tahiti Tourisme highlights clear lagoon diving and marine encounters around Moorea. [French Polynesia](/destinations/french-polynesia/) is in its warmer, wetter season in December.

## Also worth considering

The [Seychelles](/destinations/seychelles/) and [Mauritius](/destinations/mauritius/) for polished island luxury, and [Costa Rica](/destinations/costa-rica/) as its dry season begins.

## Book the experience, not just the room

Festive travel is expensive. Make the premium spend count: private boat, exceptional dinner, spa day, surf guide or a memorable dive. A beautiful room is nice. A beautiful story is better. See every option in the [December planner](/best-by-month/december/).
