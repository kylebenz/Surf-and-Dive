## January is about escaping the European calendar

January is one of the easiest months to make a strong case for an ocean escape. The question is not whether Europe has surf. It does. The question is whether you want cold-water adventure or warm-water recovery.

If you want to surf in boardshorts, start looking toward the Indian Ocean, Southeast Asia, Central America and selected islands. If a wetsuit and a short flight suit you better, the [Canary Islands](/destinations/canary-islands/) and [Madeira](/destinations/madeira/) are in their winter-swell season.

## The Maldives for a resort-led escape

[The Maldives](/destinations/maldives/) is compelling when you want the surf trip to feel like a holiday. Its official tourism site describes reef surfing and a broad season, while northern atolls are among the most accessible surf areas.

## Sri Lanka for variety

[Sri Lanka's](/destinations/sri-lanka/) official tourism information highlights a long coastline with numerous surfing and diving locations.

That variety is useful for a longer January trip, especially when you want to combine surf with food, culture, wildlife and inland travel.

## Costa Rica for surf and nature

[Costa Rica's](/destinations/costa-rica/) Pacific coast is typically in its dry season in January, with cleaner, often smaller conditions that suit learning and improving, and rainforest, wildlife and wellness for the rest of the day.

## The luxury decision

In January, we would prioritise weather and water first, then hotel. A beautiful resort cannot fix a destination that is wrong for the type of surf you want.

Build a simple shortlist: warm water, suitable wave level, good accommodation, manageable transfers and enough non-surf experiences to make the trip worthwhile. See every January option in our [January planner](/best-by-month/january/).
