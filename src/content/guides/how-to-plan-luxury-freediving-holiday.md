## Freediving is not a holiday competition

The ocean rewards patience. The biggest mistake is arriving at a beautiful destination and deciding you need to prove something.

Your holiday should make you a better, calmer freediver — not push you into a depth or breath-hold that you are not trained for.

## Choose qualified support

Book instructors or guides with appropriate credentials and local knowledge. Follow the safety system used by your training organisation and the local operator. Never freedive alone.

Destinations with established schools — [the Philippines](/destinations/philippines/) around Panglao and Moalboal, [Bali's](/destinations/bali-nusa-penida/) Amed coast — make this easier.

## Conditions come first

Wind, current, boat traffic, visibility and water temperature all affect a session. The most beautiful day on Instagram may not be the safest day in the water.

## Build recovery into the itinerary

Freediving can be physically and mentally demanding. Give yourself rest days, hydration, sleep and enough time between sessions. A luxury trip should have room for all of that.

## Leave the ocean better

Respect marine life, keep your distance, avoid touching coral and follow protected-area rules. Responsible freediving is not just safer; it is more rewarding. Before you travel, read [Ocean Safety: Freediving, Buddy Systems and Local Conditions](/guides/ocean-safety-freediving-buddy-systems/).
