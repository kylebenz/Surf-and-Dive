## Autumn is underrated

The European summer rush is over, but the ocean does not stop being interesting. Autumn can be one of the best times to travel because you have more flexibility, a calmer atmosphere and different surf seasons opening up.

## Portugal gets interesting

[Portugal's](/destinations/portugal/) coastline offers an enormous variety of surf environments, including Ericeira, Peniche and Nazaré.

For surfers, autumn can be especially appealing when Atlantic swells become more meaningful. Exact conditions vary by coast and year.

## Madeira for a mixed itinerary

[Madeira](/destinations/madeira/) is useful when you want surf without making the whole holiday about surf. Its tourism board highlights multiple surf zones with different characteristics. [The Azores](/destinations/azores/) still have warm water in early autumn, too.

## Long-haul alternatives

As Europe cools, Indian Ocean and tropical destinations become increasingly attractive. [The Maldives](/destinations/maldives/) has a long surf season, while [Sri Lanka's](/destinations/sri-lanka/) coast offers multiple water-sport areas.

## Why autumn works

You are not fighting the calendar. You can build the holiday around the ocean rather than school holidays, peak-season prices and crowded beaches. Explore [September](/best-by-month/september/), [October](/best-by-month/october/) and [November](/best-by-month/november/).
