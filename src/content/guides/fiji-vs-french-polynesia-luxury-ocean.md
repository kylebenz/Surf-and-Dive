## Two very different Pacific dreams

Both destinations deliver the postcard version of the Pacific, but the reasons to choose them are different. [Fiji](/destinations/fiji/) is especially compelling for serious surf and reef adventure. [French Polynesia](/destinations/french-polynesia/) is exceptional for lagoons, dramatic scenery and underwater experiences.

## Fiji for surfers

Tourism Fiji describes the Mamanuca Islands as a major surf area, with Cloudbreak and a range of other breaks for different abilities.

If the trip is built around surfing, Fiji has a very strong case.

## French Polynesia for underwater scenery

Moorea offers clear lagoons, coral ecosystems and marine life including rays, turtles and sharks.

The combination of mountains, lagoons and luxury accommodation creates a different kind of ocean holiday: slower and more cinematic.

## For couples

French Polynesia may have the edge if you want the landscape and romance to dominate. Fiji can win if one partner is a serious surfer and the other enjoys resort and marine activities.

Choose Fiji when the wave is the reason for the trip. Choose French Polynesia when the entire island-and-lagoon experience is the reason. Compare all twelve criteria in [Fiji vs French Polynesia](/compare/fiji-vs-french-polynesia/).
