## Start with the season, not the destination

European surfers have a useful advantage: you can choose between Atlantic surf close to home and warm-water destinations farther away. The mistake is picking a place because it looks perfect in July and only later discovering that July is the wrong month for the waves you want.

[Portugal](/destinations/portugal/) and the [Canary Islands](/destinations/canary-islands/) are natural starting points for shorter trips. For winter sun, the Canaries can make sense when you want to stay in Europe. For a major warm-water escape, destinations in the Indian Ocean, Southeast Asia and the Pacific become more interesting.

## Portugal is hard to beat for variety

Portugal packs a remarkable amount of surf into one coastline. Visit Portugal highlights Ericeira, Peniche, Nazaré, the Algarve and other areas, with different wave environments and skill requirements.

Ericeira is especially attractive for a premium trip because the town itself is part of the experience. You are not simply renting a room near a break; you have seafood, cafés, coastal walks and a strong surf culture around you.

## For winter sun, look south

The Canary Islands and [Madeira](/destinations/madeira/) are particularly useful for European travellers who want Atlantic scenery without a long-haul flight. Madeira's tourism board highlights surf areas including Jardim do Mar, Paul do Mar, Ribeira da Janela and Porto da Cruz, with conditions ranging from beginner-friendly to advanced.

That makes these islands useful for couples where one person surfs and the other would rather hike, eat, swim or explore.

## When the long-haul trip is worth it

[Bali](/destinations/bali-nusa-penida/) is one of the strongest all-round choices when you want surf plus food, culture, villas and diving. [Fiji](/destinations/fiji/) is the choice when the wave is the centrepiece. [The Maldives](/destinations/maldives/) is ideal when you want surf days wrapped inside a resort-and-reef holiday. [Costa Rica](/destinations/costa-rica/) works particularly well if you want to combine surf with rainforest, wildlife and wellness.

The key is to choose the destination around the trip you want, not around an Instagram photo.

### A simple way to decide

| If you have… | Consider… |
| --- | --- |
| A long weekend | Portugal, the Canaries or Madeira |
| A week in winter | The Canaries, Madeira, or Sri Lanka and the Maldives if you can stretch to ten nights |
| Ten to fourteen nights in summer | Bali, Fiji or the Mentawai Islands |
| A mixed-interest couple | Bali, Madeira or Portugal |
