## The best surf trip is one you both enjoy

If one person surfs and the other does not, do not choose a destination where the non-surfer spends seven days looking at the same beach chair.

Choose a place with enough depth away from the break.

## Bali is an obvious candidate

[Bali](/destinations/bali-nusa-penida/) offers surf alongside food, wellness, culture, villas and nearby island experiences. Official tourism information shows a wide range of surf locations and levels.

## Portugal is another strong option

Ericeira and the surrounding coast combine surf culture with cafés, seafood, walking, local towns and access to Lisbon and Sintra. Visit Portugal describes Ericeira as a recognised surfing reserve. See our [Portugal guide](/destinations/portugal/).

## Madeira for the active couple

One person can surf while the other hikes, swims, explores viewpoints or enjoys a spa. [Madeira's](/destinations/madeira/) dramatic geography makes the destination work beyond the surf session.

## Costa Rica for nature lovers

In [Costa Rica](/destinations/costa-rica/), rainforest walks, wildlife, yoga and spa days give a non-surfer real substance while the surfer chases the morning glass.

## The decision test

Ask: If the surfer could not surf tomorrow, would the other person still have a great day? If the answer is yes, you probably chose well.
