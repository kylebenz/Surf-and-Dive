## Beginner surf trips should feel easy

Your first surf holiday should not be an endurance test. You need manageable waves, good instruction, equipment that works and enough time to improve without pressure.

Luxury helps because recovery matters. After a session, you want a comfortable shower, good food, somewhere quiet and ideally another activity that does not involve falling off a board.

## Portugal offers enormous choice

[Portugal](/destinations/portugal/) has a huge range of surf environments. Visit Portugal highlights places including Ericeira and Peniche, with different spots suited to different abilities. Summer is typically the friendliest season for learners.

## Fiji can work beyond the famous waves

Cloudbreak is not a beginner destination, but the Mamanuca Islands contain other waves. Tourism Fiji specifically lists beginner-to-advanced options such as Swimming Pools and Tavarua Rights under certain conditions. [Fiji](/destinations/fiji/) suits beginners who are happy to learn carefully with a guide and enjoy the islands in between.

## Bali for surf plus everything else

[Bali](/destinations/bali-nusa-penida/) is attractive for beginners because the island can provide surf instruction alongside food, wellness and cultural experiences. Indonesia's official tourism site identifies Kuta, Seminyak, Legian and Sanur among beginner-oriented surf areas.

## Also worth a look

[Costa Rica](/destinations/costa-rica/) has forgiving beach breaks and established schools around Tamarindo and Nosara. [Sri Lanka's](/destinations/sri-lanka/) Weligama Bay is one of Asia's most popular places to learn in season.

## What to book

Look for small-group lessons, qualified instructors, beginner-friendly beaches and accommodation close enough that you can surf without turning every session into a logistics project. Before you go, read our [surf safety guide](/guides/ocean-safety-luxury-surf-travellers/).
