## February is made for warm water

If your idea of February involves grey skies and a wetsuit, there is another option: leave winter behind and build the month around warm water.

For a luxury dive trip, February is particularly attractive when you can combine reliable underwater interest with comfortable accommodation and plenty of surface-time activities.

## The Maldives

[The Maldives](/destinations/maldives/) remains a natural candidate for reef-focused diving, and Fuvahmulah is particularly associated with pelagic encounters and diverse marine life. February typically falls in the drier north-east monsoon.

## French Polynesia

Moorea offers a different experience. Tahiti Tourisme highlights clear lagoons, coral ecosystems and marine life including stingrays, turtles and sharks. February is in [French Polynesia's](/destinations/french-polynesia/) warmer, wetter season, so plan flexibly.

## Also worth considering

[The Philippines](/destinations/philippines/) is typically in its drier months, the [Seychelles](/destinations/seychelles/) and [Mauritius](/destinations/mauritius/) offer warm lagoons and resort comfort — check cyclone forecasts for the southern Indian Ocean.

## Build the trip around your dive priorities

Do you want sharks? Coral gardens? Walls? Pelagics? Macro life? Easy resort diving? Once you answer that, your destination list gets much shorter.

Then check operator quality and local conditions. A luxury dive holiday should feel well organised without becoming over-scheduled. Leave space for the destination itself. More options in our [February planner](/best-by-month/february/).
