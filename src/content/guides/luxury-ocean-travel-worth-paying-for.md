## Luxury is not the room size

A larger room is nice. But on an ocean holiday, the best upgrades often improve your relationship with the water.

Private boat access, an excellent guide, a room steps from the ocean, flexible scheduling and genuinely good food can change the trip more than another 30 square metres.

## Pay for access

If a private boat gets you to the right surf or dive site when conditions are good, that can be more valuable than an upgraded minibar. In [the Maldives](/destinations/maldives/), [Fiji](/destinations/fiji/) and the [Mentawai Islands](/destinations/mentawai-islands/), boat access often defines the quality of the trip.

## Pay for expertise

A great surf coach or dive guide can turn a good day into a memorable one, and help you avoid the mistakes that can ruin one.

## Pay for recovery

A good bed, quiet room, massage, healthy food and easy transport are not glamorous in photographs, but they are the things you feel after several days in the ocean.

## Do not pay for labels

A five-star label does not automatically mean an ocean experience is excellent. Look at location, access, service, activity quality and the details that matter to your trip.

| Usually worth it | Often not |
| --- | --- |
| Private boat time when conditions are good | A bigger room you rarely use |
| A top surf coach or dive guide | Brand names without water access |
| Location steps from the water | Packages full of unused extras |
| Sleep, food and recovery | Status for its own sake |
