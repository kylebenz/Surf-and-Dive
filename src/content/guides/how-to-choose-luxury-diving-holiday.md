## The resort is only half the decision

A beautiful room cannot compensate for poor dive logistics. Start with the underwater experience and work outward.

Ask what you want to see, how often you want to dive, whether you prefer reefs or pelagics, and whether you prefer shore diving, day boats or liveaboards.

## Operator quality matters

Look for qualified staff, appropriate safety procedures, well-maintained equipment and clear communication about conditions. Read current reviews, but also check the operator's own information and local requirements.

## Choose the right amount of diving

Four dives a day sounds exciting until day three. Luxury is having the freedom to stop. Leave time for a long lunch, a spa treatment, a beach walk or simply doing nothing.

## Think beyond visibility

Visibility gets too much attention. Current, marine life, reef structure, water temperature, boat time and seasonal conditions can matter just as much.

## Your booking checklist

Marine-life goal → season → skill level → operator → site access → accommodation → non-dive activities → travel logistics → safety.

If you need a starting point, [the Maldives](/destinations/maldives/) and [Fiji](/destinations/fiji/) combine strong resort diving with comfort; [the Philippines](/destinations/philippines/) offers the widest variety; [French Polynesia](/destinations/french-polynesia/) rewards divers comfortable with current.
