## Two ways to build the trip

A surf resort gives you a base. A charter gives you mobility. The better choice depends on whether you want a beautiful place to return to or more opportunities to chase the forecast.

## Choose a resort when...

You are travelling with a partner or family, want spa and dining options, prefer land-based comfort or want an easy mixed-activity itinerary.

## Choose a charter when...

You are travelling primarily to surf, want access to multiple breaks and are comfortable making the ocean the centre of the schedule. In places such as the [Mentawai Islands](/destinations/mentawai-islands/) or [the Maldives](/destinations/maldives/), specialist boat-based trips can be particularly attractive.

## The luxury trade-off

A charter can be luxurious in its own way: fewer transfers, direct access and a small group. A resort offers a different luxury: space, privacy, food and the ability to do nothing.

| | Resort | Charter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Couples, families, mixed interests | Dedicated surf groups |
| Wave access | Breaks within reach of the base | Moves with the swell |
| Comfort | Space, spa, dining | Small cabins, direct access |
| Flat days | Plenty to do | Fishing, snorkelling, reading |

## Ask one question

If the surf forecast went flat for two days, which option would still make you happy? That answer tells you more than any booking page.
