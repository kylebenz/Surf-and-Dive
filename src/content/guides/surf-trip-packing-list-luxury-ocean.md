## Pack for the water, not the Instagram photo

A luxury surf trip still needs the boring things. Rash guard, reef-safe sun protection, appropriate surf equipment and a way to protect your phone from water are more useful than another outfit.

If you are travelling with your own board, check airline rules and local transport before leaving home.

## Water kit

Board or rental plan, leash, fins, wax suited to water temperature, rash guard, wetsuit if required, reef-safe sunscreen and a compact changing towel are the basics.

- Board (or a confirmed rental) and a padded board bag
- Leash, fins and wax suited to the water temperature
- Rash guard and, where needed, a wetsuit of the right thickness
- Reef-safe sunscreen and a hat
- Reef boots for shallow reef breaks, for example in [Bali](/destinations/bali-nusa-penida/) or [the Mentawai Islands](/destinations/mentawai-islands/)
- A small first-aid kit for reef cuts
- A compact changing towel or poncho

## Recovery kit

Hydration bottle, light layers, comfortable footwear, sleep essentials and anything you normally use for muscle recovery can make a bigger difference than another pair of shoes.

## Luxury does not mean overpacking

Choose a small wardrobe that works for breakfast, beach, dinner and a nice hotel. The ocean should be the star, not your suitcase.

## The final check

Destination climate → water temperature → board availability → airline rules → local equipment rental → medical/travel essentials → weather forecast.
